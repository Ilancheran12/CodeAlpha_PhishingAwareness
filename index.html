STOP PHISHING ATTACKS

Think Before You Click. Stay Safe Online.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers use fake emails, messages and websites to steal sensitive information such as passwords, bank details and personal data.

⚠ Never trust unknown links!

Always verify the sender before sharing information.

Types of Phishing Attacks

Email Phishing

Fake emails designed to steal personal information.

Smishing

Phishing attacks through SMS messages.

Vishing

Fraudulent phone calls used to steal information.

Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing attacks against specific individuals.

Phishing Email Examples

Example 1: Fake Bank Alert

Subject: Your bank account will be blocked!

Message: Verify your details immediately.

Warning: Urgent threats are a common phishing tactic.

Example 2: Fake Prize Message

Subject: Congratulations! You won a prize.

Message: Provide your personal details to claim it.

Warning: Unexpected prizes may be scams.

Example 3: Fake Password Reset

Subject: Your password has expired.

Message: Click an unknown link to reset your password.

Warning: Check the website address independently.

How to Stay Safe Online

✓ Use strong passwords.

✓ Enable two-factor authentication.

✓ Verify email addresses.

✓ Avoid suspicious links.

✓ Never share OTP or passwords.

✓ Keep software updated.
